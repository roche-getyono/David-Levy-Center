# Product

## Register
brand

## Name
מרכז דוד לוי / David Levy Center

## Users
**Primary:** Hebrew-speaking Israelis — general public, families from development towns, Mizrahi community members, academics and educators, journalists, politicians.
**Secondary:** English-speaking international audience — diaspora Israelis, journalists, researchers, foundations, international donors.

**Psychographic:** People who feel the periphery was never given its due. People who know the name but not the full story. People who want to honor someone who fought for them without knowing it.

## Product Purpose
A hybrid memorial landing page and organizational presence. The site accomplishes two things simultaneously:
1. **Legacy** — Tell David Levy's story with the weight and permanence it deserves. A man who rose from a Moroccan construction worker to Foreign Minister. The voice of the Mizrahi millions who were overlooked.
2. **Movement** — Soft call-to-action to join, support, and participate in the foundation's ongoing work.

## Primary Conversion Goal
Email capture / membership signup. The visitor should leave feeling moved and wanting to stay connected.

## Language
Hebrew primary (RTL), English secondary. All navigation, hero copy, and key sections in Hebrew. English toggle available.

## Brand Personality
- **Grounded, Tenacious, Dignified**
- Voice: Spare and direct. Let the historical weight speak. No marketing language. No "empowering communities" filler.
- Tone: How a eulogy is delivered by someone who knew the man personally — specific details, no abstractions.

## Anti-references
- Generic Israeli NGO sites (white background, Heebo font, blue/white flag colors, stock photos of sad children).
- "Memorial museum" cliché: dark, heavy, grief-first, museum-archive feel.
- Political party aesthetics (Likud blue, flag imagery, rally-poster fonts).
- AI-default warm-cream editorial sites (Fraunces serif + mono labels + ruled columns).

## Design Principles
- **"The Classified File, Declassified"**: Dark, authoritative, serious. This is not a mourning page. It is a reckoning.
- **Periphery as Hero**: The visual system honors his Moroccan roots and development-town identity — warm amber against deep dark, not cold institutional blue.
- **One Portrait, One Story**: A single decisive photographic moment anchors the site. Everything else serves it.
- **RTL-first**: The site is designed as Hebrew-first RTL. The English version is an adaptation, not a translation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA compliant contrast in dark mode.
- Reduced motion support; animated hero must have a static fallback.
- RTL/LTR layout system must be robust — no content bleeding or alignment breaks on language switch.
